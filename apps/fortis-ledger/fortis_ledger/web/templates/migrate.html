Move a client from Xero

Before you cancel the Xero file, export:

  1. Chart of accounts - Accounting > Chart of accounts > Export. Keeps the client's own codes, names and report codes.
  2. A trial balance at each year end you need (at least last year and this year) - Reports > Trial Balance, set the date to the year end, then Export > Excel. The year is read from the report's "As at" date.
  3. Optional, for the file: General Ledger Detail and Journal Report for each year, and any attachments you need to keep. Store them in the client's folder in SharePoint.

Each trial balance becomes one import journal at that year end, so every year's closing balances match Xero exactly. Later years post only the difference from the balances carried forward. If any year fails, nothing is kept.
